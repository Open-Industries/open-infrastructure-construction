# Domain Standards — Infrastructure & Construction

Alignment mapping for this core. *Aligned to* throughout: BIM authoring, structural
calculation and statutory inspections stay with certified tools and professionals.

## Concept mapping

| Platform concept | Domain standard | Mapping |
|---|---|---|
| Projects & assets | ISO 19650 information management posture | Project/asset registries follow the common-data-environment discipline |
| Building/infra elements | IFC (ISO 16739) | Element identifiers are adopter IFC GUIDs at the boundary |
| Progress certification | Contract administration practice | Guarded contract-value accounts; the no-overrun invariant |
| Safety | ISO 45001 | Activity safety rules with reasoned decisions; site risk scoring |
| Risk | ISO 31000 | Risk posture in governance and scoring functions |

## Scope boundaries

This core does not produce engineering calculations, BIM models or safety certifications;
it is the project/valuation/safety-rules platform around certified professional work.
